# Design Direction

## Creative Direction
Studio hardware meets modern digital storefront.

## Visual Principles
- Dark technical base
- Bright accent channels by category
- Structured lane system with clear hierarchy
- High readability over decoration

## Color Tokens (Initial)
- Background base: #0F1115
- Surface 1: #161A21
- Surface 2: #1D2330
- Text primary: #EAF0FF
- Text muted: #9AA6C5
- Accent green: #5CFF95
- Accent cyan: #43D8FF
- Accent amber: #FFBF47
- Accent red: #FF5A70

## Typography
- Display/headings: condensed and industrial sans family
- Body/UI text: clean sans optimized for legibility
- Numeric labels/prices: monospaced or tabular numerals

## Motion Guidelines
- Lane entry: short staggered slide-up on initial load
- Card hover: subtle lift and edge glow
- Carousel interaction: smooth snap and momentum

## UI Components to Define
- Track Lane
- Product Card
- Price Badge
- Category Tag
- CTA Buttons
- Detail Panel
- Cart Drawer

## Accessibility Notes
- Ensure contrast ratio targets are met
- Never encode meaning with color alone
- Keyboard focus states must be visible on all controls
